Rewatched 07 Oct 2025

When it comes to the Rule of Cool, this is my favourite film ever.

The most visually striking I’ve ever seen, over the hardest score I’ve ever heard. I can’t fathom how they thought to elevate the 1982 original to this unique, modernized continuation, and then executed it perfectly, too.

The visuals on the Grid are immaculate. Sleek, sporty, nonchalant vibes; all convolved in glossy black metal and ribbons of light. Breathtaking CGI for 2010, and not a single chance…
