TRON: Legacy

2010
Rewatched 07 Oct 2025

My favourite film ever.

The most visually striking I’ve ever seen, over the hardest score I’ve ever heard. 

I can’t fathom how they thought to elevate the 1982 original to this unique, modernized continuation, and then executed it perfectly, too.

The grid has such an immaculate vibe. Sleek and nonchalant and sporty and competitive.

All fights are awesome. All the whips are done right.
